Guitars, amplification and touring setup

Gear and equipment.

A practical overview of equipment planning for live, studio, dep and touring work.

Project-ready equipment

The right setup for the music, venue and brief.

Equipment choices are made around the job rather than using one fixed setup for everything. Live shows, recording sessions, dep work, theatre-style productions and touring can all require a different balance of instruments, amplification, effects and backup options.

When making an enquiry, include any required guitar types, tunings, amplifier arrangements, silent-stage requirements, supplied backline and travel limitations. That makes it easier to plan an appropriate and reliable rig.

A model-by-model equipment list should only be published once the exact current inventory has been confirmed. Detailed availability can be discussed for a specific booking.

Equipment areas

What a project may require.

Electric guitars

Instrument choices can be matched to tuning, sound, visual brief, playing style and backup requirements.

Acoustic instruments

Acoustic or electro-acoustic options can be planned where the arrangement or performance requires them.

Amplification

Traditional amplifier, supplied backline and lower-volume or direct approaches can be discussed according to the production.

Pedals and effects

Effects can be organised around the repertoire, patch changes, stage space and practical reliability.

Recording setup

Recording requirements, file formats, references, revisions and delivery expectations should be agreed before a remote or studio session.

Tour-ready planning

Cases, spares, power, transport, changeovers and backup plans matter when equipment is travelling between shows.

Before the booking

Useful equipment questions.

  • Is backline supplied at the venue or studio?
  • Is the stage silent, low-volume or using conventional amplifiers?
  • Are there alternate tunings or specialist guitar sounds?
  • Are flights, restricted luggage or shared transport involved?
  • Is a duplicate or backup signal path required?
  • Are in-ear monitors, click tracks or playback systems being used?
