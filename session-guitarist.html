Session services

Guitar parts that support the song and the job.

I provide session guitar for live performances, recording sessions, rehearsals, dep work and touring projects. The priority is to understand the musical brief, prepare properly and play what the song or production needs.

Projects can range from a one-off studio date or performance to repeat artist work and multi-date touring. Reference tracks, charts, demos, set lists and musical direction can all be shared before the booking.

My tour-management experience also gives me a strong understanding of schedules, production communication and professional conduct around a busy show day.

Session guitar FAQs

Before making a booking.

Where is Billy based?

Billy is based in South Wales and can discuss work elsewhere in the UK depending on the project.

Can Billy learn an existing set?

Yes. Send recordings, live versions, charts, notes and set lists as early as possible.

Does Billy do dep work?

Yes. Dep work can be discussed for rehearsals, performances and touring where the material, schedule and expectations are clear.

How are fees agreed?

Fees depend on preparation, rehearsals, performance or recording time, travel, equipment requirements and the number of dates.
