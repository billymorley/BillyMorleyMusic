Session services

I learn the set, then I get out of its way.

Live shows, recording sessions, rehearsals, dep work, touring — send me the tracks or the charts and I’ll learn what the song actually needs, not just what I want to play. I can read notation, TAB, chord charts and Nashville numbers, learn by ear, transpose, harmonise, improvise, arrange and sing backing vocals, so however the material arrives, I can work with it.

I’ve stepped in overnight to learn a full set for a gig the next day, and I’ve had years to prepare for others — either way, I show up knowing the material. Send me reference tracks, charts, demos or a set list and I’ll take it from there.

My tour-management background also means I understand schedules, production communication and how to behave on a busy show day without adding to anyone’s stress.

Session guitar FAQs

Before making a booking.

Where are you based?

South Wales, but I’m up for talking about work anywhere else in the UK too.

Can you learn an existing set?

Yes — send me recordings, live versions, charts, notes and set lists as early as you can, and I’ll be ready.

Do you do dep work?

All the time. Give me a clear brief and I can usually be ready with very little notice — sometimes overnight.

How are fees agreed?

Depends on prep, rehearsals, performance or recording time, travel and how many dates are involved — send me the details and I’ll quote properly.
