Session services

Guitar parts that support the song and the job.

I provide session guitar for live performances, recording sessions, rehearsals, dep work and touring projects. The aim is always to arrive prepared, understand the musical brief and contribute the right part rather than simply play more.

Projects can range from a one-off performance or studio date to repeat work with an artist or production. Musical direction, reference tracks, charts, demos and set lists can all be discussed in advance.

How it works

A straightforward process from enquiry to performance.

1. Send the brief. Include dates, location, set length, rehearsal requirements and any reference material.

2. Confirm the details. We agree availability, expectations, fee, travel and what needs to be prepared.

3. Prepare properly. Tracks, charts, notes and arrangements are reviewed before the session or rehearsal.

4. Deliver professionally. The focus stays on the artist, the music and helping the wider team do its job.

Session guitar FAQs

Before making a booking.

How far do you travel?

I am based in South Wales and can discuss work elsewhere in the UK, with travel and accommodation agreed as part of the booking.

Can you learn an existing set?

Yes. Send recordings, live versions, charts, notes and any arrangement details as early as possible.

What styles do you cover?

Send the project references and musical brief. Suitability can then be confirmed honestly before committing to the work.

How are fees agreed?

Fees depend on the work involved, preparation, rehearsals, performance time, travel and the number of dates.
