Learn your way

The point is to make you want to keep playing.

Every student is different, so every lesson plan is different — there’s no fixed structure I force everyone through. I’ll get a feel for where you’re at, why you want to play and what excites you about the guitar, then build lessons around that.

The biggest mistake teachers make is pushing the instrument so hard it stops being enjoyable. The biggest mistake students make is not practising. My job is to help with the first one and give you the motivation to sort the second — usually by teaching songs you actually like, not ones out of a book.

Whether that’s your first three chords, exam prep, or getting back into it after years away, lessons can be as structured or as relaxed as suits you.

Lesson FAQs

Common questions from new students.

Do I need experience?

None at all. We’ll start with the basics — setup, first chords, rhythm, a few simple songs — and go from there.

Am I too old to start?

No one is. It won’t happen overnight, but put the work in and you’ll get there — I’ve taught students from 12 to 75.

Do you teach children?

Yes, subject to availability and a chat about age, goals, location and format first. I’m DBS checked.

Are online lessons available?

Yes, if you’ve got a decent device, connection and space to play.
