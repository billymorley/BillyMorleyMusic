One-to-one guitar tuition

Practical guitar lessons built around your goals.

Supportive tuition for beginners, developing players and adults who want to improve their technique, confidence and understanding of music.

Ask about lessons

Learn your way

Lessons with a clear purpose.

Guitar lessons should help you make real progress while keeping you interested in playing. Each lesson is shaped around your current level, the music you enjoy and the skills you want to develop.

That might mean learning your first chords, improving rhythm, understanding the fretboard, developing lead playing, preparing songs, building confidence or becoming a more complete musician.

What lessons can cover

Useful skills you can apply to real music.

Beginners

Comfortable foundations, tuning, basic chords, rhythm, simple songs and good practice habits.

Technique

Cleaner fretting, picking, timing, coordination, bends, vibrato and control.

Rhythm guitar

Strumming, groove, chord changes, dynamics and playing confidently with recordings or other musicians.

Lead guitar

Phrasing, scales, improvisation, ear training and creating parts that sound musical.

Music theory

Practical theory connected to the fretboard, songs, chords, keys and improvisation.

Song learning

Work through music you genuinely enjoy while identifying the techniques and ideas inside it.

Teaching approach

Supportive, structured and realistic.

There is no single route that suits every guitarist. Lessons can be structured closely for someone who wants a clear programme, or kept more flexible for someone learning for enjoyment.

The important part is that each lesson has a useful outcome and gives you something manageable to work on between sessions.

Progress is built through regular practice, honest feedback and material that is challenging enough to help without becoming discouraging.

Lesson FAQs

Common questions from new students.

Do I need any experience?

No. Complete beginners are welcome, and lessons can start with the basic setup and first steps.

Do adults take lessons?

Yes. Lessons are suitable for adults starting for the first time, returning after a break or looking to improve specific skills.

What guitar do I need?

An electric or acoustic guitar can both work. Mention what you currently own when making an enquiry.

How much do lessons cost?
