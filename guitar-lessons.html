How I teach

Four things every lesson should give you.

Lessons are tailored to your musical interests, experience and goals, while still giving you enough structure to make clear progress.

01

Learn music you actually like

Songs and styles are chosen around your interests, so practice feels connected to the reason you picked up the guitar.

02

The right level of challenge

Enough structure to keep you improving, without making lessons frustrating or taking the enjoyment out of playing.

03

Understand what you’re playing

Technique, theory and fretboard knowledge are explained practically and applied to real music.

04

Leave knowing what to practise

You finish each lesson with a clear, realistic plan that fits the time you have available.

Your first lesson

A relaxed starting point with a clear plan.

  1. 01

    Talk about your experience and musical interests

    We’ll discuss what you already know, the music you enjoy and what you would like to achieve.

  2. 02

    Play something together and identify a suitable starting point

    This helps me see what feels comfortable and where the most useful next step is.

  3. 03

    Agree a few achievable goals

    We’ll choose clear targets that suit your experience, interests and available practice time.

  4. 04

    Leave with something useful to practise

    You’ll have a focused task, exercise or piece of music to work on before the next lesson.

Your goals

What would you like to be able to do?

Lessons can focus on a practical musical outcome rather than working through disconnected exercises.

  • Play complete songs rather than disconnected exercises
  • Jam confidently with other musicians
  • Improve timing and rhythm
  • Understand the fretboard
  • Prepare for a performance or grade
  • Return to guitar after a long break
  • Play and sing at the same time
  • Develop a reliable practice routine

Who lessons are for

Tuition for different ages and starting points.

Complete beginners

Start with tuning, posture, simple chords, rhythm, easy songs and useful practice habits.

Adults

Begin for the first time, return after a break or focus on the music and skills that matter to you.

Children and young people

Age-appropriate, encouraging tuition with clear communication about progress and practice.

Developing players

Improve technique, timing, fretboard knowledge, improvisation and confidence.

Grade support

Work on technical and musical skills connected with graded study where appropriate.

Returning guitarists

Rebuild confidence, fix old habits and create a realistic route back into regular playing.

Lesson FAQs

Common questions from new students.

Do I need experience?

None at all. We’ll start with the basics — setup, first chords, rhythm and a few simple songs — and go from there.

Am I too old to start?

No one is. It won’t happen overnight, but put the work in and you’ll get there — I’ve taught students from 12 to 75.

Do you teach children?

Yes, subject to availability and a chat about age, goals, location and format first. I’m DBS checked.

Where are lessons available?

I’m based in Cardiff and offer in-person lessons, online lessons and lessons at your home.

How much are lessons?

A 30-minute guitar lesson is £20 and a 60-minute guitar lesson is £35.
