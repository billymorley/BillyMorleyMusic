Rates and booking terms

Quoted around the actual work involved.

There is no single useful price for every session, lesson or tour. Preparation, duration, travel, equipment, usage, responsibility and the number of dates can all change the scope of a booking.

Rather than publishing a figure that may not apply, enquiries are quoted from a clear brief. This gives both sides a better understanding of what is included.

Exact public “prices from” figures were discussed in the original plan, but no confirmed amounts were included in the shared chat. None have been invented here.

What affects a quote

Different services involve different preparation.

Session guitar

Preparation, charts, rehearsals, recording or performance time, travel, equipment, usage and the number of dates.

Guitar lessons

Lesson duration, location, online or in-person format, travel and whether lessons are individual or recurring.

Tour management

Tour length, advancing workload, routing, party size, responsibilities, driving, travel days and pre-production.

Get a useful answer

Send enough information to quote accurately.

Include the service, dates, location, expected timings and the responsibilities or outcome you need. For music work, share tracks, charts or set information. For touring, include routing and party size.
