Tour management

Clear plans, clear communication and fewer surprises.

Touring involves a large number of moving parts, and small problems become expensive when information is unclear or decisions are delayed. My role is to help keep the artist, crew, venues and suppliers working from the same plan.

Support can begin before the first show with advancing and preparation, then continue on the road with schedules, logistics, settlements, merchandise and day-to-day problem solving.

My experience includes worldwide touring, giving me an understanding of the practical demands placed on artists and crews across changing venues, travel days and production environments.

Tour support

Practical help across the full run.

Show advancing

Confirming venue contacts, timings, access, technical details, hospitality and local arrangements.

Itineraries and schedules

Clear daily information for travel, hotels, load-in, soundcheck, doors, show and curfew.

Travel and accommodation

Coordinating routes, transport, parking, hotels and practical touring requirements.

Venue liaison

A central point of contact between artist, crew, promoter, venue and local staff.

Settlements and paperwork

Supporting settlements, documentation, receipts, expenses and agreed reporting.

Merchandise

Organising stock, venue arrangements, sales information and reconciliation where required.

Before the tour

Preparation that protects the show day.

  • Confirm venue and promoter contacts.
  • Check access, parking, load-in and production timings.
  • Build practical schedules and travel plans.
  • Confirm hotels, rooming information and transport.
  • Identify missing information and realistic contingencies.
  • Keep the touring party working from current information.

Tour management FAQs

Planning the right level of support.

Do you work on short tours?

Yes. Enquiries are welcome for one-off shows, short runs and longer touring projects, subject to availability.

Can advancing be included?

Yes. Advancing can be included where venue, promoter and production contacts are supplied in good time.

Do you have international experience?

Yes. Billy’s experience includes worldwide touring as well as UK-based projects.

Can driving be part of the role?

Transport and driving requirements should be included in the enquiry so the role, vehicle and schedule can be assessed properly.

What should I send first?

Send the dates, routing, venues, number of people, transport plan and the responsibilities you need covered.
