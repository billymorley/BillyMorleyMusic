Tour management

You can’t stress on tour — if you do, the whole band feels it.

Touring has a lot of moving parts, and small problems get expensive fast when information is unclear or decisions get left too late. My job is to keep the artist, crew, venues and suppliers all working off the same plan — and to stay calm doing it, because if the tour manager’s stressed, the band’s stressed, and that affects the show.

That can start before the first date with advancing and preparation, then carry on through the run with schedules, logistics, settlements, merchandise and whatever the road throws at us — that's the job, really: sort it, stay calm, get everyone home happy.

My touring experience covers the UK, Europe and further afield, so I know what changing venues, travel days and different production setups actually demand.

Tour support

Practical help across the full run.

Show advancing

Confirming venue contacts, timings, access, technical details, hospitality and local arrangements.

Itineraries and schedules

Clear daily information for travel, hotels, load-in, soundcheck, doors, show and curfew.

Travel and accommodation

Coordinating routes, transport, parking, hotels and practical touring requirements.

Venue liaison

A central point of contact between artist, crew, promoter, venue and local staff.

Settlements and paperwork

Supporting settlements, documentation, receipts, expenses and agreed reporting.

Merchandise

Organising stock, venue arrangements, sales information and reconciliation where required.

Before the tour

Preparation that protects the show day.

  • Confirm venue and promoter contacts.
  • Check access, parking, load-in and production timings.
  • Build practical schedules and travel plans.
  • Confirm hotels, rooming information and transport.
  • Identify missing information and realistic contingencies.
  • Keep the touring party working from current information.

Tour management FAQs

Planning the right level of support.

Do you work on short tours?

Yes — one-off shows, short runs, long tours, all of it, subject to availability.

Can advancing be included?

Yes, as long as venue, promoter and production contacts are shared with enough time to work with.

Do you have international experience?

Yes — touring across Europe and further afield as well as UK dates.

Can driving be part of the role?

Yes, I’ve got my own transport and can drive — just flag it in the enquiry so we can sort the right vehicle and schedule.

What should I send first?

Dates, routing, venues, how many people, the transport plan and what you actually need me to cover.
