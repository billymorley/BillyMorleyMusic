Frequently asked questions

Useful information before getting in touch.

Where are you based?

I am based in South Wales and can discuss work elsewhere in the UK depending on the project.

What information should I send?

Include the service you need, dates, location, expected timings and any music, set lists or project details already available.

Do you take one-off jobs?

Yes. Enquiries are welcome for one-off sessions, performances, lessons, short runs and longer touring projects.

How quickly will you reply?

I aim to respond as soon as possible. Time-sensitive enquiries should include the date clearly in the subject line.
