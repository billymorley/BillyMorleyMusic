Guitarist, teacher and tour manager. Still obsessed with all three.

I’m Billy Morley. I picked up a guitar at about six because of Elvis, then Hendrix ruined me for anything else — and fifteen-odd years later I’m still doing this for a living: playing sessions and shows, teaching students of every age, and keeping tours running smoothly. I turn up prepared, I work hard, and I’m genuinely easy to be around.

Experience

Hundreds of gigs a year, for going on fifteen years.

I’ve played everything from rooms with two people in them to festival stages in front of thousands — Glastonbury, Green Man, Festival No.6 and the Eisteddfod among them — plus TV and radio work through S4C and the BBC, and touring across the UK, Italy, France and the USA.

Frequently asked questions

Before getting in touch.

Where are you based?

South Wales, but I’m happy to talk about work anywhere in the UK, and touring further afield if it fits.

What details should I send?

The service you’re after, the dates, location and roughly what’s involved. Any tracks, charts or routing info you’ve already got helps too.

Do you take one-off work?

Definitely. A single session or lesson is just as welcome as a long tour or a regular teaching slot.

How are prices agreed?

It depends on the job — prep, travel, gear, how many dates. Send me a proper brief and I’ll come back with an accurate number.
