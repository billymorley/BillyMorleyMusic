Frequently asked questions

Before getting in touch.

Where are you based?

I’m based in South Wales. Enquiries are welcome for local work, UK-wide projects and suitable touring work further afield.

What details should I send?

Include the service, dates, location, expected timings and any music, routing or project information already available.

Do you take one-off work?

Yes. Enquiries are welcome for individual sessions, performances and lessons as well as repeat work and longer touring projects.

How are prices agreed?

Pricing depends on preparation, time, travel and the exact requirements. Send a clear brief for an accurate response.
