Guitarist, teacher and tour manager.

I’m Billy Morley. I’ve wanted to play guitar for as long as I can remember — first because of Elvis, then Hendrix — and twenty-odd years later I’m still doing what I love for a living: playing alongside some of the best musicians around, teaching students of every age, and keeping bands on the road with tours that run smoothly. I spent over ten years working in music retail alongside all of that too, and I still can’t get enough of it.

What I do

Showing up prepared is the baseline.

Fifteen years in, that's the least you should expect. What actually makes the difference is the rest of it — enthusiasm, being easy to work with, and someone who can keep things light even when the day gets stressful.

Frequently asked questions

Before getting in touch.

Where are you based?

South Wales, but I’m happy to talk about work anywhere in the UK, and touring further afield if it fits.

What details should I send?

The service you’re after, the dates, location and roughly what’s involved. Any tracks, charts or routing info you’ve already got helps too.

Do you take one-off work?

Definitely. A single session or lesson is just as welcome as a long tour or a regular teaching slot.

How are prices agreed?

It depends on the job — prep, travel, gear, how many dates. Send me a proper brief and I’ll come back with an accurate number.
