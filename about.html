About Billy Morley

A working musician with a practical approach.

Session guitar, one-to-one teaching and tour management all require different skills, but the professional standards behind them are the same.

Background

Music on stage, in lessons and behind the scenes.

I'm Billy Morley, a working musician based in South Wales. My professional work covers three connected areas: session guitar, guitar teaching and tour management.

That combination means I understand projects from several perspectives. I know what musicians need to perform well, what students need to keep progressing and what touring teams need to keep a show moving.

Across each part of my work, I aim to combine musical awareness with the preparation, communication and practical thinking needed to make a project run well.

Whether the job is on stage, in a lesson or behind the scenes, the goal is to provide reliable support and contribute positively to the wider team.

Working principles

What clients and students can expect.

Preparation

Learning the material, understanding the brief and checking practical details before the work begins.

Communication

Clear, timely information so artists, students, venues and crew know what is happening.

Professionalism

Being punctual, dependable, adaptable and respectful of the wider team and project.
