About Billy Morley

I've wanted to be a musician for as long as I can remember.

Being in this industry my whole working life has led me into a few different things I'm good at and enjoy — play, teach and manage tours. Three different jobs that all come from the same place: I love music, I'm good with people, and I like being useful.

Background

Elvis started it. Hendrix made it permanent.

I've been playing for years now, and started proper lessons in secondary school with John Rodge — the same teacher who taught Peredur ap Gwynedd (Pendulum) and Joe Harvatt. I always knew I wanted to be in a band, and music has been the thing I've built my whole working life around since.

These days that means session guitar, teaching, tour managing and even more — live shows, studio sessions, TV and radio appearances, and touring on stages ranging from tiny rooms to festival fields.

Doing all three gives me a genuinely useful perspective: I know what a band needs from a guitarist, what a student needs from a teacher, and what an artist needs from the person running their tour. Preparation, clear communication and sorting the practical stuff — that's the whole job, really.

Selected experience

Some of the good bits.

Session and touring work with HMS Morris, Adwaith, Carwyn Ellis and Monaco, plus TV and radio appearances through S4C, BBC Radio Cymru and BBC Radio Wales. HMS Morris picked up the SUNS Europe festival prize and were runners-up for the Welsh Music Prize, and one of my proudest moments to date was supporting Super Furry Animals on their 2016 comeback tour.

The exact role and scope of work varies between projects — happy to talk through relevant experience for your booking.

How I work

What clients, artists and students can expect.

I actually prepare

Charts written, tracks learned, questions asked in advance — so I show up ready, not winging it. I get what it's like, whatever your situation is — I've been there. Whether you want it to sound exactly like the record or something with a different flavour, just tell me and I'll work around you. I play for the music, not for myself.

I treat you like a person

Friendly, approachable, and genuinely listening to what you need — that goes for a nervous first-time student or a headline artist.

If you’re early, you’re on time

I turn up with time to spare, adapt to whatever the day throws at me, and I’m easy to have around.
