About Billy Morley

A working musician on stage and behind the scenes.

I combine more than 15 years of playing, formal music training, teaching experience and worldwide touring knowledge across three connected areas of professional work.

Work with Billy

Background

Performance, teaching and touring.

I’m Billy Morley, a South Wales-based session guitarist, guitar teacher and tour manager. I hold a Bachelor of Music and Grade 8 Guitar, and I’m DBS checked for teaching work.

My experience includes live performance, recording, television and radio appearances, artist projects and worldwide touring.

Working in several parts of the music industry gives me a wider understanding of what makes a project successful: musical preparation, clear communication and the practical details all matter.
